❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Ahem Sawalat)

1. CryptoWorld signals kis tarah generate hotay hain?

Hamara automated engine real-time Binance Futures API se 250 candles fetch karta hai aur RSI, MACD, EMA 20/50/200, ADX aur ATR calculate karne ke baad AI neural network aur quantitative quality gates se guzarta hai.

2. Free account mein rozana kitne signals miltay hain?

Free account ko rozana 1 high-probability signal milta hai jo har raat 12:00 AM PKT par reset ho jata hai. Agar aap dost refer karein toh har refer par +2 signals permanent miltay hain.

3. NO_TRADE ya WAIT aane par mera quota cut hoga?

Nahi! Hamare fair-use policy ke tehat agar market choppy ho aur system NO_TRADE ya WAIT ka mashwara de toh sirf 0.25 quota deduct hota hai taake apka pura signal zaya na ho.

4. Premium plan kaise active karein?

Aap dashboard ke Premium button ya WhatsApp par rabta karke premium license key haasil kar sakte hain. Key milne ke baad "Redeem Premium Code" mein enter karein aur account foran upgrade ho jayega.

5. Stop Loss aur Take Profit follow karna kyun zaroori hai?

Crypto futures mein risk management sab se pehla qanoon hai. Hamara system hamesha minimum 1:1.5 Risk/Reward ratio maintain karta hai taake Stop Loss hit hone par bhi apka portfolio stable rahe.

6. Device Lock feature kya hai?

Fraud aur abusive multi-accounting rokne ke liye aik phone ya computer par 7 din mein sirf 1 naya account register ho sakta hai. Pehle se bana hua account kisi bhi waqt login ho sakta hai.

7. Kya CryptoWorld ko Android ya iPhone par install kar sakte hain?

Jee haan! Yeh aik progressive web app (PWA) hai. Chrome mein "Install App" button click karein, ya Safari mein Share button daba kar "Add to Home Screen" select karein.

8. Outcome Tracking kaise kaam karti hai?

Har LONG/SHORT signal automatically journal mein save hota hai. Har 30 minute baad system Binance klines check karta hai aur verify karta hai ke pehle TP hit hua ya SL, aur win-rate dashboard ko update karta hai.
